Before you open Clever

Clever is a separate service. Loading the widget connects your browser to Clever. If you submit text in the widget, Clever receives it and retains the text and results for 24 hours. The widget owner can review its usage history. Keep sensitive writing in the local editor.

The Writing Review editor keeps its text separate. You choose what to paste into Clever.
